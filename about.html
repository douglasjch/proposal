About Us

IRS special agents came to my door for an interview around 1980. My father was terminated after filing tax form W-4, and I seen friends lose homes. This sent me on a quest to better understand the constitutional issues so I enrolled in Linfield College (Class of 90') to sharpen my research skills and eventually brought suit to the IRS.

Working with the Union I was able to pass this resolution in statewide convention in Oregon.
